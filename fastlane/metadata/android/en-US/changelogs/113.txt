Streak 2.0.0 🚀

- Streak runs on Linux now, as an AppImage or a tar.gz
- Focus keeps going outside the app, with the time left at the top of the screen
- Skip the break, add a minute, tap anywhere to pause, double tap for a full screen clock
- A session that crosses midnight counts on both days
- Reorder your categories, and a new Plan tab for your day
- No vibration on every tap, and no more swiping into the wrong tab
- A faster start and faster statistics
- Fixed the graph axis, the hidden Save button and a phantom Focus alarm
