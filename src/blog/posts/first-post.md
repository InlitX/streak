Hi, and welcome. This is the first post on the Streak blog, so it seemed right to start by explaining what this space is for.

## What you will find here

Most posts will be about things you might need while using Streak. Some examples:

- **Setting it up your way.** Installing it, moving your data, or keeping your habits on your phone and your PC at the same time without using the internet.
- **Getting more out of it.** Features that are easy to miss and small tricks that make the app fit your day better.

Every now and then I will also write about building better habits, studying, or improving yourself a little. This is not a course and I am not an expert. It is just open, personal advice: things that have worked for me and that might be useful to you too.

## What this blog is not

It will not fill up with posts just for the sake of it. I will only write when there is something worth saying, and anything that explains a step by step will be tested first, with real screenshots, before it goes up.

## That is all for now

This was just a first post to show how the blog will work. Over time I will add more, always with some link to Streak. If there is something you would like me to write about, leave a comment below.

Thanks for reading.
