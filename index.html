Everything, nothing more

Built to feel calm, not demanding

One-tap logging

Mark today done from the home screen — daily, weekly or monthly goals with a habit “strength” that tracks your consistency.

Activity heatmap

A GitHub-style grid for every habit — week, month or year at a glance.

Three habit types

Build good ones, quit bad ones, or hit an amount.

  • Normal
  • Avoid
  • Amount
New

Checklists

Break a habit into steps — the day is done only when every step is ticked. Perfect for a morning routine.

  • Wash face
  • Brush teeth
  • Stretch 5 min
New

Vacation mode

Pause a habit while you’re away. Skipped days don’t count as missed and never break your streak.

Make it yours

Icons, emoji, a full color picker, light/dark themes and home-screen widgets.

Statistics

Trends, totals and the times of day you’re most consistent.

Smart reminders

Per-habit notifications on exactly the days you choose.

Backup & restore

Export everything to a portable JSON file. Your data, in your hands.
